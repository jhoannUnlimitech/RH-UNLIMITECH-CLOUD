# Design — RH Unlimitech Cloud Video Corporativo

## Brand
- **Company:** Unlimitech Cloud
- **Product:** RH Unlimitech Cloud — Sistema de Gestión de Recursos Humanos
- **Tone:** Profesional, moderno, confiable, accesible
- **Audience:** Líderes de RRHH, CTOs, Founders de empresas tech medianas

## Motion

- Easing: power3.out (entrances), power2.in (exits)
- Duration: 0.4-0.7s typical, 1.0s for hero elements
- Stagger: 80-120ms between siblings
- Ambient: 8-12s breathing on decoratives (scale 1.0-1.03)

## Depth

- Subtle shadows, no hard drops
- Accent glows at 15-25% opacity
- Layered backgrounds with radial gradients

## What NOT to Do

- No gradient text
- No neon/cyberpunk aesthetic
- No stock photo vibes
- No all-caps body text
- No cluttered screens (max 5 elements visible at peak)
